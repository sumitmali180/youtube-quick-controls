# Quick Plan: Idle Auto-Hide Behavior for Control Rows

## Objective
Make both Speed and Quality control rows behave like native YouTube player overlays: appear immediately when the mouse moves over the YouTube video player, smoothly fade out after ~2.5 seconds of inactivity across normal and fullscreen modes, stay visible while hovering/interacting/keyboard-focusing or while a quality switch/tooltip is active, and reappear instantly on mouse movement without affecting video playback or shifting YouTube's Like button layout.

## Implementation Steps
1. **Controller Auto-Hide State Machine ([`youtube-quick-controls/content.js`](../../../youtube-quick-controls/content.js)):**
   - Add `IDLE_HIDE_DELAY_MS = 2500` and idle/interaction tracking (`idleTimer`, `isIdleHidden`, `isPointerInsideHost`, `isPointerDownInsideHost`, `isKeyboardFocusedInsideHost`, `activityAbort`, `isEmittingKeepalive`).
   - Add `bindActivityTargets()`, `showControlsOnActivity()`, `scheduleIdleHide()`, `clearIdleTimer()`, `setIdleHidden()`, and `isInteractionActive()`.
   - Bind lightweight throttled `mousemove` / `pointermove` / `pointerenter` listeners on the active player containers (`#movie_player`, `.html5-video-player`, active Shorts player, `<video>`) and toolbar mount parent (`anchor.mountParent`).
   - Pause/prevent auto-hide while hovering or pressing either row, during keyboard `:focus-visible` focus inside controls, while `isQualitySwitching` is pending, or while `.yqc-tooltip` is visible; resume idle countdown cleanly when interaction finishes, and clean up timers/listeners on unmount/navigation/destroy.
2. **Smooth Fade & Reduced-Motion Styling ([`youtube-quick-controls/styles.css`](../../../youtube-quick-controls/styles.css)):**
   - Transition compositor-friendly `opacity` smoothly (`140ms ease-out` fade-in, `280ms ease-in` fade-out inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`), applying `opacity: 0; pointer-events: none` when `[data-yqc-idle='true']:not(:has(:focus-visible))` or fullscreen `.ytp-autohide` applies so zero layout shift occurs on YouTube's Like toolbar and video playback is unaffected.
3. **Tests & Documentation:**
   - Update `tests/unit-manifest-bridge.test.mjs` and `tests/e2e-chrome-dom.test.mjs` to test real ~2.5s idle fade-out, instant wake on `#movie_player` mouse movement, hover/focus interaction hold across both rows, and fullscreen parity; update `youtube-quick-controls/README.md`.
