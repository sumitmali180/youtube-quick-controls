# Summary: Video Display Controls (`261125-u12`)

## What Changed

1. **[`youtube-quick-controls/content.js`](../../../youtube-quick-controls/content.js)**:
   - Added `DISPLAY_MODES` (`fit`, `stretch`, `fill`, `original`) and `displayMode` controller state (default `'original'`).
   - Rendered `#yqc-display-row` (`role="group"`, `aria-label="Video display"`) as the top row inside `#yqc-controls-host`, directly above `#yqc-speed-row` and `#yqc-quality-row` (and above YouTube's Like/player controls across Watch, Fullscreen, and Shorts).
   - Added `clearVideoDisplayOverrides()`, `applyVideoDisplayElement()`, `renderDisplayRow()`, and `applyDisplayMode()` to toggle `yqc-video-display` and `data-yqc-display` on the active `<video>` immediately on click with `aria-pressed` active-state indicators, preserve the active mode across Watch <-> Fullscreen transitions on the same video, reset cleanly to `'original'` on new video navigation, and remove overrides on unmount/destroy.
   - Generalized `installToolbarKeyboardFallback` `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` navigation across all `.yqc-row` groups.
2. **[`youtube-quick-controls/styles.css`](../../../youtube-quick-controls/styles.css)**:
   - Added `.html5-video-player .html5-video-container:has(> video.yqc-video-display[data-yqc-display])` and `video.yqc-video-display[data-yqc-display='fit'|'stretch'|'fill']` rules applying `object-fit: contain`, `object-fit: fill`, and `object-fit: cover` respectively at `100% × 100%` container sizing while leaving `'original'` untouched.
3. **Tests & Documentation**:
   - Updated [`tests/unit-manifest-bridge.test.mjs`](../../../tests/unit-manifest-bridge.test.mjs), [`tests/e2e-chrome-dom.test.mjs`](../../../tests/e2e-chrome-dom.test.mjs), [`youtube-quick-controls/README.md`](../../../youtube-quick-controls/README.md), and [`README.md`](../../../README.md).
